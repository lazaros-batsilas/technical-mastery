# My journey towards becoming an AI Engineer

## Introduction

This blog documents my efforts to consolidate my knowledge of conventional software engineering and artificial intelligence, with the goal of becoming a competent AI engineer.

I begin this journey with professional experience in Java and Spring Boot, as well as self-taught skills in Python, traditional artificial intelligence and mathematics.

After experiencing some frustration and uncertainty in light of the latest developments in AI, I decided to use ChatGPT to help me establish a structured learning tutorial.

## My Learning Objectives

My objective is to build upon my existing software engineering experience while developing the skills required to design, implement and maintain reliable AI-powered applications.

The programme will cover:
- Advanced Python engineering
- Software engineering practices
- Database engineering
- Backend development
- Linux, Docker and DevOps
- Data engineering
- Machine learning
- Large language model engineering
- Retrieval-augmented generation
- Production AI systems

## Why I Created This Blog

I am documenting my learning journey for two reasons.

First, I want to create a personal knowledge base that allows me to track my progress, revisit important concepts and learn from my mistakes.

Second, I hope that making this material publicly available will prove helpful to others pursuing a similar learning path.

## My Learning Approach

Each lesson will combine theoretical knowledge with practical implementation. Wherever possible, I will include code examples, technical explanations, common pitfalls and personal observations.

Artificial intelligence will serve as a learning assistant rather than a substitute for independent thinking and practical experience.

## Disclaimer

The articles and code examples published on this blog are intended for educational purposes. Although I aim to ensure their accuracy, they are provided without any guarantee of correctness, completeness or safety.

## Looking Ahead

My first technical article will focus on setting up a professional Python development environment on Linux.

From there, I will gradually progress towards more advanced software engineering and applied AI topics, documenting both my achievements and the difficulties I encounter along the way.
